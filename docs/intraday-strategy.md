# Intraday Strategy Research

TradePilot's first intraday research candidate is an Opening Range Breakout with trend, volume and volatility confirmation.

## Filters

- Opening range breakout
- 9/20 EMA trend alignment
- Current-bar volume relative to the prior 20-bar average
- ATR-based stop and target
- Extreme opening-gap rejection
- Risk-based position sizing
- Maximum capital allocation per trade
- No overnight carry in the intraday backtester

This is a research strategy, not a claim of profitability. The next validation stages are Indian-market historical data, realistic costs/slippage, walk-forward testing, out-of-sample testing and paper trading.
